FIRST CONTACT—Predicting shot
What’s behind the simulation?

Equal solid spheres (radius 28.575 mm) on a 2.54 × 1.27 m table. Fixed 1 ms steps track linear velocity and three spin components. Cloth slip transitions to rolling; collision impulses include restitution and Coulomb friction. Side spin affects ball contact and cushion rebound.

This is an educational approximation: no pockets, jump shots, cue elevation, squirt, swerve, or cloth grain. Rails use a simplified vertical contact model. Prediction and playback share the same engine; simulation stops at rest or 30 seconds.

Model background: The physics of pool — Evan Kiefl. Independent implementation.
