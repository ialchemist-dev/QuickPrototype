# ATTENTION RETURNED — AI Personal Secretary
## Production handoff for the Mac mini agent

**Status:** production-ready creative specification; no video has been generated.
**Project:** `ai-personal-secretary-film`
**Owner approval required:** API spending, account registration, publishing, and any external order.
**Target:** 120–150 seconds, 16:9, 1920×1080 final master, 24 fps, English dialogue, cinematic live-action realism.
**Creative direction:** Option A — an ordinary person's day, told like a film, **not** a product demo.

### 1. Creative intent and constraints

A powerful personal AI secretary gives people their attention back. The protagonist does not open apps, navigate menus, or stare at a phone. A discreet finger snap, ring tap, or natural spoken phrase can initiate conversation; the secretary handles coordination in the background and requests explicit human approval before consequential actions.

**Thesis:** De-interface + Attention Sovereignty + Human Sovereignty / Bounded Delegation.
**Emotional arc:** everyday friction → effortless orchestration → deliberate human choice → presence.
**Visual language:** warm, naturalistic, elegant, believable contemporary life. Restrained, intimate camera movement; realistic sound; no holographic UI, futuristic HUD, floating chat windows, glossy corporate montage, or magic claims.
**Avoid:** claiming fully autonomous purchases without consent, revealing private data, showing branded services without clearance, implying this exact system already works reliably in production.
**Recurring visual motif:** the protagonist's hands are free to live, work, cook, and connect — never occupied by a phone.
**Tagline:** “The interface disappears. The agency remains yours.”
**End card:** “ATTENTION RETURNED.” A secondary line may read “Technology should give your attention back.”

### 2. Narrative structure

Six sequences across one ordinary day: morning briefing; a birthday gift; coffee and lunch; a disrupted schedule; a meaningful work decision; and an undisturbed evening. The assistant voice is calm, concise, warm but not servile. No celebrity likeness or imitation of an identifiable actor's voice. All characters are fictional adults. Use a single consistent protagonist and a single consistent secretary voice.

**Protagonist:** adult in their early thirties, contemporary understated wardrobe, thoughtful, approachable; exact appearance must be locked from a reference sheet before generation.
**Secretary:** voice-only, natural conversational English, gender-neutral or subtly feminine, understated. No physical avatar.
**Setting:** contemporary US city, apartment, sidewalk, neighborhood cafe, office, home. No legible sensitive screen contents.
**Sound:** natural room tone and restrained music; dialogue should remain clearly audible. Prefer dialogue and ambience assembled in post-production for continuity.

### 4. Visual continuity bible

- Establish a **character reference sheet** before generating motion: front, three-quarter, profile, consistent hairstyle, skin tone, clothing; obtain approval before any paid video runs.
- Lock apartment kitchen geometry, ring design, recurring wardrobe, daylight progression, cafe exterior/interior, office, and dinner location.
- Film palette: soft morning gold, neutral daytime daylight, cooler rain/office tones, warm dusk and evening. Natural lenses (35/50 mm equivalent), shallow but not excessive depth of field.
- Hands must be anatomically correct; the ring should not switch hands or morph.
- Keep the AI **diegetic and invisible**: voice without an avatar. No phone unlocks, app browsing, or fake holograms.
- Never render substantial readable text inside AI-generated video; add title card and any essential written details in editing.
- Avoid real company logos, product trademarks, recognizable celebrity likenesses, and copyrighted film footage.
- Shoot dialogue-heavy shots with mouths not clearly visible when practical; perform voice-over in post to avoid lip-sync artifacts.

### 5. Generation plan: model-agnostic, Seedance first

**Do not assume any model version, API endpoint, pricing, US availability, clip-duration limit, or audio feature is current.** The Mac mini agent must check official provider docs and its selected US-accessible provider *at execution time*, document source links, model identifier, pricing units, terms, and availability. Prefer Seedance when available and suitable; if not, compare viable alternatives and request approval before changing provider.

Generate **short shots individually**, rather than asking a model for the entire film. Each shot has a 4–9 second target; split shots that exceed the selected model's supported length. Use reference images or reference-video features only if explicitly supported by the chosen API. Native video audio is optional; final dialogue should normally be mixed in post.

Workflow:
1. Verify provider access, API billing, terms, output rights, content rules and cost estimates. No paid generation until approved.
2. Build a continuity bible and **3–5 style/character/location reference images**; obtain human sign-off.
3. Produce a low-cost **animatic** (stills + timed dialogue + temporary music), matching the 19-shot timing. Obtain approval before final video generation.
4. Generate a **pilot of shots 02, 07, 14 and 18** to test face/wardrobe consistency, ring action, city/cafe realism, and dialogue-heavy scenes.
5. Review pilot; only then render remaining shots. Reuse locked prompts, seeds/references when supported.
6. Assemble, record or synthesize original non-impersonating voice, add licensed music and SFX, mix and export.
7. Perform automated QC **and actual human viewing/listening** before calling the film approved.

### 7. Audio and edit

- English dialogue from section 3 is canonical. Use a consistent original AI voice and a distinct natural human voice. Do not imitate identifiable public figures.
- Voice style: low-key, believable conversational pacing, with pauses and room ambience. Avoid robotic “assistant demo” delivery.
- Cut to the **emotional rhythm**, not equal-length clips. Dialogue can begin before a visual cut (J-cut) or continue across it (L-cut).
- Original or properly licensed music only. Store license/source/attribution metadata.
- Aim for dialogue intelligibility and consistent perceived loudness; avoid clipping.
- Deliver: `final_1080p_24fps.mp4` (H.264 + AAC), `review.mp4`, `captions_en.srt` (separate optional accessibility captions, not burned in), `final_script.md`, `shot_manifest.json`, and `credits_and_licenses.md`.
- Preserve a clean version without end-card text when feasible.
